### 🚀 About Me

I'm a software engineer who brings user-centric, data-driven product thinking to every project. I got here through four years in digital marketing, most recently at **Yellow Pages Kenya**, where I worked on client websites struggling with poor visibility and stagnant traffic.

I ran technical SEO audits, rebuilt and optimized landing pages using **React** and **Next.js**, and fixed the structural issues underneath — resulting in a **65% improvement in average organic rankings** and an **85% increase in organic traffic** within six months, with **90% client retention**.

That project is where I realized I loved *building* the solution more than promoting it — so I made a deliberate choice to go deeper into engineering fundamentals: **JavaScript, React, Next.js, Node.js, and MongoDB**, learning to build things myself rather than relying on AI to do it for me.

What makes me different from most junior developers is that I already know how a product needs to perform for real users and real businesses, because I've spent years measuring exactly that. I'm looking for a role or mentorship where I can sharpen my core engineering skills while contributing that product instinct from day one.
